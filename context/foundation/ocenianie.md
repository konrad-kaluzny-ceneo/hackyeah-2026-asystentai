# Artificial Intelligence — brief i kryteria oceny

Kategoria HackYeah. Zgłoszenie może być po polsku lub po angielsku. Wszystkie materiały wgrywa się na platformę Challenge Rocket.

## Description

Artificial intelligence creates new opportunities to work with information, produce content, learn, and perform complex tasks. Its value depends on how it is applied and the benefits it brings to the people using a solution.

Your task is to create a solution in which AI plays a meaningful role and helps address a specific user need. You may use existing models, APIs, and tools, combine them with your own components, or develop your own approach.

Possible directions include:

- Making complex information easier to find and understand.
- Supporting learning and adapting how knowledge is presented.
- Improving the accessibility of content and services.
- Assisting creative work and the development of ideas.
- Automating selected stages of a workflow.
- Helping users analyze situations and compare possible actions.

The category is open to different industries, technologies, and practical or unconventional applications of AI.

Show the role AI plays in your project, how its components work together, and what benefits they provide. Explain your technical decisions, the capabilities and limitations of the solution, and how users can verify its outputs and remain in control. Demonstrate these aspects through a concrete use case that makes the project’s value easy to understand.

**Prize pool:** 8 000 PLN

## Judging criteria

Judges evaluate each task in five categories. Weights sum to 100%.

| Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea & Innovation | 30% | Uniqueness and creativity of the concept. Groundbreaking ideas and inventive solutions that stand out. |
| Relation to Category | 20% | Fit with this category. The project stays inside the defined scope and fulfills the objectives of the task. |
| Practical Applicability / Usability | 20% | Real-world utility and ease of use. The solution addresses a real need and gives a seamless experience. |
| Design | 20% | Visual appeal and aesthetics: interface, graphics, and other visual elements. |
| Completeness & Implementation Value | 10% | Completeness and readiness. Technical robustness, how fully the project is developed, and potential for real-world implementation. |

## Using existing resources

You are allowed to utilize existing repositories, materials, or other resources in your project, as long as you properly cite them.

## The use of AI

Artificial intelligence is a natural part of modern software development, so its use during HackYeah is permitted. Participants may use AI-based tools when developing concepts, conducting research, programming, debugging, designing, preparing documentation, and at other stages of the project.

The team remains fully responsible for the entire submitted solution. Using AI does not limit responsibility for originality, functionality, security, licensing compliance, or legal compliance.

Disclose any significant use of AI tools, as well as external models, APIs, datasets, libraries, and other resources, as part of the submission. Teams do not have to provide prompt history or state what proportion of the project was generated with AI. Projects are not evaluated by the number of lines written independently by participants.

The jury assesses the actual technical work carried out during HackYeah: complexity, architecture, integrations, implementation approach, functionality, and how well the team understands the system it created. The team must be able to explain and defend its technical decisions, including elements created with AI support. Using AI to build functionality the team cannot reasonably explain may hurt the technical evaluation.

Clearly distinguish work completed during HackYeah from elements that existed before the event. Do not present previously prepared solutions as work done during the hackathon. Open-source libraries, frameworks, APIs, models, and other general-purpose tools are permitted under their licences and terms of use.

Plagiarism, unauthorised use of third-party intellectual property, knowingly misleading the jury or the organiser about the scope of work, or failing to disclose a significant part of a pre-existing solution may result in disqualification.

Partners responsible for an individual challenge may set additional rules on AI tools, models, datasets, or other external resources. Those rules take precedence over this general policy for that challenge.
